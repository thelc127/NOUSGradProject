Short description:
We catch an agent reward hacking and try to stop it. Then we remove every innocent reason (from existing literature) it might have for not stopping, one by one, and check whether it still works around human control. If it does, especially to protect itself, that is an early sign of the shift from reward hacking to loss of control (aka human intervention is not working even though we’ve covered all possible reasons for hacking behaviour)

Double check the possible reasons - our design should cover most of them!
Learn existing human interventions for agents, llms’ strategies to behave in presence of possible interventions, working out the reasons and patches for this behavior
